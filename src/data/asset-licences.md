# Asset licences

Every third-party asset used in the app or its render pipeline, and how it is credited.

## Does CC BY-SA need a credit? Yes.

The muscle model (Z-Anatomy, based on BodyParts3D, via the Gymmetry muscle map) is CC BY-SA 4.0
(BodyParts3D itself: CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan). CC BY-SA requires, wherever the work or an adaptation
of it is shared (the app counts):

1. **Attribution**: name the creators and source, link the licence.
2. **Indicate changes**.
3. **ShareAlike**: adaptations are shared under the same licence.

Both the Muscles tab model (`assets/models/muscles.glb`) and the clay renders are covered: the renders bake the
model's muscle detail (normal map) and region ids into the MakeHuman body, so they are adaptations and are
shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. MakeHuman (CC0) needs no credit, but we credit it anyway.
free-exercise-db (Unlicense) and the OFL fonts need no in-app credit; they are listed for completeness.

**Where the credits are:** Settings → Credits (in-app, with sources and the changes made),
`assets/models/CREDITS.md` (next to the model), `render/README.md` and this file.

## Exercise videos (real people)

35 exercises have a short looping video of a real person. All come from the open exercise
database **wger** (wger.de; exercise videos contributed under Creative Commons). Every clip used here is
**CC BY-SA 4.0** by the wger contributor **Goulart**. They were chosen one by one (right exercise and
equipment, clear view, good form), trimmed to a 3–6 s loop, cross-faded at the loop point, muted and
re-encoded at the original resolution and frame rate. As adaptations they are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0
(the same licence). Credited in Settings → Credits and under each exercise's video. No videos from
YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or other gym apps are used. Exercises without a video show the clay still
images (or the free-exercise-db photo). Videos you record or import stay in the app's storage on your
phone only; they are never bundled or uploaded.
