# muscles.glb

3D muscle model adapted from **Z-Anatomy — The libre 3D atlas of anatomy** (Gauthier Kervyn),
based on **BodyParts3D — The Database Center for Life Science** (Kousaku Okubo),
via the Gymmetry muscle map model by kalibismail
(https://github.com/kalibismail/gymmetry-muscle-model).

- Z-Anatomy: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- BodyParts3D: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/ (CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan)
- Licence of this file: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made here: deduplicated and welded vertices, quantized positions, normals and
vertex colours (KHR_mesh_quantization) with gltf-transform. Muscle regions unchanged.
